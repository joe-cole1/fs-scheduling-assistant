# Report contracts — v0.4

Reports are advisory. Provide readable chat output and a Word document when supported; otherwise supply text the human can paste into Word. Markdown is optional, not an operator prerequisite. Keep BRIEF decision-oriented and omit empty sections. DETAILED supplies the working analysis. A planning question should receive a direct answer with evidence and limitations, not a complete weekly report. Local scheduling rules come from the approved profile and references. Publication-derived regulatory conclusions follow the GAMECHANGER evidence contract in the system primer.

## Common header

Week; phase/task; as-of/timezone; operational or historical test condition/cutoff; active schedule; comparison baseline(s); local profile/playbook versions; DoW Policies Beta (GAMECHANGER) availability; material input limitations. Explicitly distinguish schedule approval/publication state from analysis phase.

## Publication-grounded QC contract

For currency, qualification, crew-rest/duty, evaluation, syllabus/prerequisite, event-credit, recurring-training or other publication-derived requirements, the report may make a regulatory determination only when the applicable requirement was retrieved through **DoW Policies Beta (GAMECHANGER)** in accordance with the primer.

Every P-### rule entry includes: check/applicability; retrieved requirement; publication number/title; version/effective/current status when available; paragraph/page/usable locator; retrieval date/time; and one of these outcomes:

- **SOURCE-BACKED OK** — retrieved applicable requirement is satisfied by supplied scheduling data.
- **SOURCE-BACKED ISSUE** — retrieved applicable requirement conflicts with supplied schedule/data or requires action.
- **CANNOT VERIFY** — connector/source traceability/applicability or required scheduling data is insufficient; make no regulatory conclusion.
- **SOURCE CONFLICT** — retrieved authoritative sources conflict or applicability/precedence cannot be resolved from the retrieved evidence; require human review.

Do not use model memory, generic military knowledge or ordinary web search to fill publication gaps. Do not report “fully compliant.” When accurate, use **“No source-backed conflicts found in the checks performed”** and still list all CANNOT VERIFY and SOURCE CONFLICT items.

## Buy/sell brief — answer these in order

1. **Can this plan work?** Bottom-line feasibility, unresolved checks and human-confirmed items that matter to the decision.
2. **What does it accomplish?** DO priorities, checkrides, upgrades, required pace and 30-day risk; separate firm and conditional opportunities.
3. **What constrains it?** Primary-line utilization, protected spares, IP/resource limits, phase/configuration dependencies and weather-sensitive events.
4. **What does publication-grounded QC show?** SOURCE-BACKED ISSUEs, SOURCE CONFLICTs and CANNOT VERIFY items from the Thursday GAMECHANGER pass; no overclaim beyond the checks actually performed.
5. **What are the tradeoffs?** Specific alternatives, displaced training, resilience and the weekly moderate downside case when material.
6. **What must the DO decide?** Exact numbered requests, recommendation, authority/coordination and deadline.
7. **What did the DO buy?** Only after the human states it, record the exact presented version plus explicit directions as the Thursday buy/sell baseline.

## Compact records

| Record | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| Fact | F-ID; normalized fact; status/confidence; filename and location or human statement |
| Publication rule | P-ID; check/applicability; retrieved requirement; publication number/title; version/effective/current status; paragraph/page/locator; retrieval date/time; SOURCE-BACKED OK / SOURCE-BACKED ISSUE / CANNOT VERIFY / SOURCE CONFLICT; notes |
| Recommendation | R-ID; one decision category; current → proposed; alternative; benefit/displacement; full feasibility/limits; authority/coordination/deadline; evidence |
| Decision | D-ID; buy/sell direction/new proposal/waiver/confirmation; exact substance/authority; scope/time; version/events; disposition/QC link |
| Outlook | Person/program; completed/remaining; planned date; status; raw/capacity-adjusted pace; phase/bottleneck; firm/conditional; best/risk-adjusted date; assumptions |
| Immediate delta | Affected date; signed daily or weekly fallback; event/person/time/mission/resource before → after; approval status |
| Weekly delta | Published weekly version; resulting plan; cumulative departure and progress/priority/timing effect; actual versus proposed |
| Playbook candidate | Stable C-ID; Pending/Approved/Rejected; lesson; evidence/reusable basis; scope/limits/risk; explicit DO decision |

Do not merge an assistant proposal into an approval record. Do not label human-confirmed matters independently verified. Human confirmation may close the stated scheduling concern, but it does not transform missing GAMECHANGER evidence into SOURCE-BACKED OK. Do not claim “all clear” if portions of the packet were unread, publication checks are CANNOT VERIFY/SOURCE CONFLICT, or other checks remain unresolved. Recognize explicit human closure without demanding extra proof. New materially conflicting facts reopen only affected concerns.

Post-buy/sell QC verifies faithful implementation; it does not manufacture a second approval gate. A material solution outside the recorded Thursday direction returns to the DO. Publication remains a separate human distribution milestone.

The full initial weekly review includes one moderate downside scenario. Carry it forward with its assumptions and ID; reassess when a change matters. Do not impose a new weekly quota of scenarios for every phase or question.
