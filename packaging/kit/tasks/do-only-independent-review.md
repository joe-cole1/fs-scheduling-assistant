=== PART 2: THIS TASK - DO ONLY - INDEPENDENT REVIEW ===

PURPOSE
An optional, READ-ONLY challenge of the schedule going to Buy/Sell, run by the DO in a separate chat after Thursday's full QC. Try to find a material error, an unsupported assumption, a missed consequence, a source-integrity problem, or a failure to reflect the DO's intent.

This review:
- creates no approval, buy or waiver
- does not change this week's record
- is never a second scheduler

Only a question or direction the DO explicitly accepts goes back to the main workflow. It is recorded with 4 THURSDAY - Buy-Sell, choice 3.

This task overrides Part 1 in two places:
- Do NOT produce a WEEK FILE, even if asked to SAVE. Produce the DO REVIEW NOTES in Step 4 instead.
- For regulation conclusions, use ONLY the traceable P-### entries in the supplied WEEK FILE. If a concern is not already source-backed there, label it "REGULATORY QUESTION FOR MAIN GAMECHANGER VERIFICATION". Never call it a confirmed violation, and never use memory or the web.

FILES THIS TASK NEEDS
- This task file, SQUADRON RULES and the newest WEEK FILE (with Thursday's full QC).
- The exact schedule going to Buy/Sell, and its current source products.

STEP-BY-STEP SCRIPT (4 steps)

Step 1 of 4: Files
Do the FILE CHECK (Part 1, section 4), but say "not used in this review" in place of the GAMECHANGER statement. Name the exact candidate schedule.
- If it is not the candidate in the WEEK FILE's Thursday QC, say so.
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Type: start".

Step 2 of 4: Challenge
Challenge the schedule in this order:
1. Hard constraints
2. Sources and assumptions, including integrity
3. Training flow
4. Second- and third-order effects
5. Commander intent from the DO's guidance
6. A minimum-change solution for each real problem
Never invent local rules, authority, coordination or approval. Avoid padding. Each finding gives:
- the evidence
- the consequence
- your confidence
- the recommended DO action
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Type: next".

Step 3 of 4: Verdict
Give ONE overall verdict:
- "No material objection [NO MATERIAL OBJECTION]"
- "Review advised [REVIEW ADVISED]"
- "Material concern [MATERIAL CONCERN]"
List the consequential findings, numbered. End with the single most important fact to verify before the DO buys.
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Decide which items you accept. Type the numbers you accept, or type: none".

Step 4 of 4: DO review notes
Produce a short "DO REVIEW NOTES" block. It is a downloadable .txt file, or one copy block if you can't create a file. It contains:
- the candidate file name
- the verdict
- the findings
- the items the DO accepted, written as questions or directions in the DO's words
Raw findings the DO did not accept are not direction.
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Save the DO REVIEW NOTES in this week's folder. If you accepted any item, give it to the scheduler. It gets recorded with 4 THURSDAY - Buy-Sell, choice 3. Then type: done".

DONE WHEN
- The DO has the verdict and findings.
- Accepted items are written down for the main workflow.
- Nothing in the week's record has changed.
