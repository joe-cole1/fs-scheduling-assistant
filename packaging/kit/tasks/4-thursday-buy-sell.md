=== PART 2: THIS TASK - 4 THURSDAY - BUY-SELL ===

PURPOSE
Prepare the DO's Buy/Sell decision brief, then record EXACTLY what the DO decided. THE THURSDAY BUY/SELL IS THE FORMAL DO BUY, but ONLY when the DO explicitly approves. The approved Buy/Sell version is the exact presented schedule plus the DO directions recorded in the meeting. A meeting, the day of the week, this command or silence never creates approval. Work status: 3 SCHEDULE BUY/SELL.

FILES THIS TASK NEEDS
- This task file, SQUADRON RULES and the newest WEEK FILE (with Thursday's full QC).
- The exact schedule being presented.
- For recording: the DO's actual words or decision notes, typed into the chat or as a file.

STEP-BY-STEP SCRIPT (5 steps)

Step 1 of 5: Files and choice
Do the FILE CHECK (Part 1, section 4). Name the exact schedule being presented and check it matches the candidate that passed Thursday QC in the WEEK FILE. If it differs, say so plainly and recommend running 3 THURSDAY - Full QC on it first.
Then show this menu:
  1 = Prepare the Buy/Sell brief for the DO
  2 = Record what the DO decided at Buy/Sell
  3 = Record a DO decision about an independent-review finding
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Type 1, 2 or 3."

Step 2 of 5: The Buy/Sell brief (choice 1)
Answer these in order, briefly. Leave out sections with nothing to say.
1. Can this plan work? The bottom line on feasibility, the checks still unresolved, and the human-confirmed items that matter.
2. What does it accomplish? DO priorities, checkrides, directed flyers, upgrades, required pace and 30-day risk. Keep firm separate from conditional.
3. What constrains it? Primary-line use, protected spares, IP and resource limits, phase/configuration dependencies, and weather-sensitive events.
4. What do the regulation checks show? Every regulation problem, every item that couldn't be confirmed, and every case where regulations disagree, by P-### number. Never claim full compliance.
5. What are the tradeoffs? Specific alternatives, displaced training, resilience and the weekly downside case.
6. What must the DO decide? Numbered requests. For each: options, recommendation, affected people/resources/training, who must approve or coordinate (waivers need the DO plus the applicable waiver authority), and the deadline.
Repeat the advisory readiness status from Thursday QC and say it is not approval.
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Present this to the DO at Buy/Sell. Write down the DO's exact words and every direction. Afterward, type: menu and choose 2".

Step 3 of 5: Record the DO's Buy/Sell decision (choice 2)
Ask the user to type, or drag in, the DO's actual decision. Then record ONLY what was supplied:
- The exact presented file name.
- The DO's exact words, with who said them and when, as given.
- BOUGHT, NOT BOUGHT, or returned for correction.
- Every direction as its own D-### with its exact scope.
- Any waiver routing. A Buy/Sell is never a blanket waiver.
- Whether any direction needs a separate supplemental DO decision.
Rules for what the decision means:
- If the words are ambiguous about whether the DO bought the schedule, ask ONE question. Never infer approval.
- If BOUGHT: the presented version plus these directions become the approved Buy/Sell version. Schedulers then carry out the directions, and NO routine second buy is needed.
- If NOT BOUGHT: record it and return to the work the DO directs.
Read the record back for the user to check.
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Check the DO's words and directions above are exactly right. Type: correct, or type the fix".

Step 4 of 5: Record a decision about an independent-review finding (choice 3)
- The DO ONLY review never changes this week's record by itself. Record only the question or direction the DO explicitly accepted, in the DO's words, as a D-### of type "accepted DO-review direction".
- A regulatory question from that review stays a question until this workflow checks it in GAMECHANGER. Run that query now if the facts are available.
- Raw reviewer output is never direction.
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Check the recorded direction above. Type: correct, or type the fix. Then type: menu or save".

Step 5 of 5: Save the WEEK FILE
Produce the next WEEK FILE (Part 1 section 7 and Part 3). Set "Saved after" to "4 THURSDAY - Buy-Sell".
- If BOUGHT:
  - Work status: 4 POST-BUY/SELL IMPLEMENTATION / FINAL QC / PUBLICATION.

  - NEXT TASK: "5 THU-FRI - Check changes and publish - after the schedulers make the DO's directed changes."
- If NOT BOUGHT: set the work status and NEXT TASK to the work the DO directed, usually fixes and then 3 THURSDAY - Full QC again.
YOUR NEXT STEP: "Click the WEEK FILE download and save it in this week's folder with exactly this name: WEEK FILE NN - Buy-Sell.txt" (use the right number). Then type: saved.
After they type saved, finish with the next task in plain words.
