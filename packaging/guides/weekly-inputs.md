# Weekly input checklist

Use existing squadron products. One file may cover several boxes. Check received items; record Missing or Not applicable and the source owner for the rest.

## Required weekly inputs

- [ ] DO priorities and directed flyers for the target week.
- [ ] Upgrade tracker, checkrides, prerequisites, due dates and recent results.
- [ ] Current currency and qualification products, Letter of Xs and callsign roster.
- [ ] Leave, commitments, non-flying duties and known future absences with exact times.
- [ ] Maintenance turns, primary aircraft, protected spares and configuration plan.
- [ ] Simulator periods, available capacity and booked events.
- [ ] Range, airspace, tanker and other support allocations, or Not applicable.
- [ ] Available weather forecast with its date and confidence.
- [ ] Training phase and planned configuration transitions.

Request these each week. A missing product leaves its affected checks unresolved; continue supported planning and follow up with its owner.

## Optional or when applicable

- [ ] Flight Commander personnel notes and additional CT preferences.
- [ ] Prior-week schedule and actual prior-duty times for boundary checks.
- [ ] Longer-term training, support, absence or capacity plans for the 30-day outlook.
- [ ] New execution results or changed sources not already reflected in the trackers.
- [ ] Additional DO decisions, restrictions, waivers or coordination records.

Supply applicable extras when they affect a check. Keep sensitive detail limited to its scheduling effect.

Upload current **Local Guidance** separately. Put drafts, presented versions and published schedules in **Schedules**. Do not fill out another tracker or invent a missing rule.
