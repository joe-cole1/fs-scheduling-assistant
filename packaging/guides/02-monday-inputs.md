# Monday — Inputs due

All scheduling inputs are due **NLT COB Monday of the prior week**. Monday is the human input deadline. It does not automatically prove the packet is complete, start analysis, or advance the assistant's internal phase.

Unless a human directs otherwise, the target is the next execution week. Source owners should identify the product's coverage or as-of date rather than make the scheduler complete a separate run-control form.

## What should be available

- Upgrade/MQT tracker and recent results.
- Checkride, currency and qualification inputs.
- Leave, commitments and known upcoming TDYs.
- Current dated Letter of Xs and callsign/name roster.
- Maintenance turn, aircraft configuration and spare plan.
- Simulator schedule.
- Range, airspace, tanker and other support allocations.
- Weather forecast when available.
- Training phase/configuration plan.
- DO weekly guidance and Flight Commander inputs.
- Stable rules, approved playbook and other governing references.
- Prior-week schedule or actual duty times when needed for boundary checks.

Use the squadron's existing products. Do not recreate trackers just for the assistant. **02 DO Weekly Guidance.docx** and **03 Flight Commander Input.docx** in System → Blank Forms are only for information not already captured elsewhere.

## Source-quality expectations

A product should be readable, identify its target-week coverage or as-of date, and distinguish missing, not-applicable and error states. A calculated workbook used for person-specific conclusions must reliably tie each displayed value to the correct person or stable identifier. Duplicate identities, broken lookups, formula errors, hidden stale data or a material identity mismatch must be surfaced rather than masked by plausible cached values.

Tuesday's assistant review classifies supplied products as **CURRENT, STALE, PARTIAL, CONFLICTED, INTEGRITY FAILED, UNREADABLE,** or **SUPERSEDED**. A limitation blocks only the affected checks; unrelated planning continues.

## Before leaving Monday

1. Confirm the expected products were requested or received.
2. Identify obvious missing owners or late inputs for Tuesday follow-up.
3. Keep products in the approved scheduling location. Do not upload operational products to GitHub.
4. Do not delay Tuesday solely because a nonblocking product is missing. The assistant will identify which checks are limited.
