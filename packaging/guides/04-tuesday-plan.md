# Tuesday — Plan the week

Use this after **03 Tuesday - Start the Week.docx**. Tuesday planning reconciles the packet and identifies what must drive Wednesday's human schedule build. The assistant continues using the inferred next execution week unless a human selected another period.

Excel, PDFs, readable screenshots, calendar exports and plain-text notes are acceptable. In a continuing chat, upload only new or revised inputs. Do not retype existing squadron products into blank forms.

## Tuesday first pass

Before the schedulers build Wednesday's lineup, answer five questions:

1. **What needs clarification?** Identify missing, stale, partial, conflicted, unreadable, superseded or integrity-failed inputs and ask only questions that materially affect planning.
2. **Which checkrides/evaluations matter this week?** Use documented due dates, future leave/TDY, prerequisites, qualified evaluators, resources and DO guidance. Distinguish a due date from a recommended earlier window.
3. **Which DV or senior-leader flyers require deliberate placement?** Identify only flyers actually directed or supported by the inputs. Do not infer qualification, priority or a flying requirement from rank/title alone.
4. **How should upgrades be planned?** Identify each upgradee's next legal events, prerequisites, desired weekly pace, completion risk, instructor/resource demand and useful candidate windows. Do not build the complete lineup.
5. **What publication-backed currency or regulatory requirements matter this week?** Use the currency, qualification, Letter of Xs, upgrade/evaluation and planned-activity products as person-specific facts only after their integrity is adequate. Query **DoW Policies Beta (GAMECHANGER)** for governing publications and build the initial **Weekly Publication Rule Ledger**. Do not use model memory or ordinary web search to establish the rule.

Also identify major aircraft/configuration, simulator, range, tanker, support, weather and personnel constraints that should shape the build.

Every publication-derived rule must identify the retrieved publication number/title plus paragraph, page or another usable locator. Record version/effective/current status when available. Use only **SOURCE-BACKED OK, SOURCE-BACKED ISSUE, CANNOT VERIFY,** or **SOURCE CONFLICT**. If GAMECHANGER cannot provide a sufficiently traceable applicable publication, make no regulatory determination.
