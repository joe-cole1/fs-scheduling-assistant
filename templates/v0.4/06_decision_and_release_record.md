For proposals, retain Pending/Accepted/Rejected/Withdrawn and link any resulting direction. These weekly statuses are separate from the enduring playbook's Pending/Approved/Rejected ledger.

Every sell direction must have a disposition. Mark new proposed changes separately; do not hide them inside corrections. A rejected proposal is not a direction to implement. A human's scoped “it's okay” can close a QC concern without additional evidence demands. Record it accurately, including role/time only when available.
