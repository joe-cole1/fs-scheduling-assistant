# Scheduling Continuity Guides

These documents are human-facing continuity references for the 35 FS scheduling process.

- **35 FS Scheduling SOP v4.0 - CAO 17 Sept 2026.docx** is the Scheduling SOP for the weekly and execution-week process.
- **Panton Weekly Scheduling QC and Buy-Sell Checklist - CAO 17 Sept 2026.docx** is the detailed weekly QC and Buy/Sell checklist.

They complement, rather than replace, the repository's current system instructions, the approved Local Profile, governing publications, or explicit DO direction. The guides are intentionally Word documents so they can be opened on normal Air Force workstations.

When the local scheduling process changes materially, deliberately review and update these continuity documents so they remain aligned with current practice.
